    Life on campus

    A 40-acre campus beside the river, with workshops open until midnight.

    Labs and workshops

    The Fabrication Hall has CNC mills, 3D printers and a welding bay. Students can book machines for personal projects, not just coursework.

    Clubs and teams

    Robotics, solar car, formula racing, a coding guild and a drone team that competes nationally every spring.

    Placements

    Over 120 companies recruit each year across software, core engineering and research. The median package last year was ₹7.2 lakh.

    Living here

    Four hostels, a central library open 20 hours a day, a sports complex, and a canteen that serves until late.
